# Business Requirement Document (BRD)

## Project Name

HR Analytics Dashboard

---

## Business Problem

The Human Resources department needs an interactive dashboard to monitor employee information, workforce distribution, salary trends, and employee attrition.

Currently, HR reports are created manually in Excel, making it difficult to identify trends and make timely decisions.

---

## Business Objectives

- Monitor employee performance
- Analyze employee attrition
- Track department-wise workforce
- Compare salaries across departments
- Improve HR decision making
- Build interactive HR reports

---

## Stakeholders

- HR Manager
- HR Executive
- Management Team

---

## Expected KPIs

- Total Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Average Salary
- Average Age
- Department-wise Employees
- Gender Distribution
- Job Satisfaction

## Scope

The project focuses on analyzing employee data to identify workforce trends, attrition patterns, salary distribution, and department-wise performance.
